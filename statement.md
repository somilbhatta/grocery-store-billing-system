PROJECT STATEMENT:
This Is a Small Grocery Store Billing System

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Small grocery often realy on paper-based reacord.Because of small grocery owner use paper-based reacord so it might be lost or damaged.So there is need a system which is run digitally,fast,lightweight no need of heavy shoftware\hardware

SCOPE OF THIS PROJECT:
-Add grocery products with name, price, and quantity.
-Store and display products in a shopping cart.
-Calculate the total bill.
-Apply discounts based on the total amount.
-Generate the final bill.
-Provide a simple menu-driven interface.

TARGET USER:
-Small grocery store owners and shopkeepers who need a simple and lightweight digital billing system.


HIGH-LEVEL FEATURE:
-Add Product: Add product name, price, and quantity.
-View Cart: Display all added products and their amounts.
-Calculate Total: Calculate the total cost of all products.
-Discount System: Apply 5%, 7.5%, or 10% discount based on the bill amount.
-Final Bill: Display the total, discount, and final payable amount.
-Menu-Driven System: Easy navigation through numbered options.
